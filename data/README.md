# Data

`raleigh_survey_2018.csv` is the City of Raleigh 2018 community survey
respondent-level data, originally published on the city's open data portal.
Each row is one respondent; the columns used in the paper are a block of
Likert/categorical items on quality of life, city services, and demographics.

`raleigh_2020_community_survey_instrument.pdf` is the 2020 wave's published
results report, kept here as a codebook proxy since it documents the same
instrument and response scales (the original 2018 open-data page is no longer
live).

Both files are public survey artifacts and contain no direct identifiers
beyond a coarse block-level latitude/longitude already present in the source
open-data extract.
